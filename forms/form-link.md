# Futurelab FDE School: Application Form

**Form link:** https://forms.gle/JrgavHXK2U2H8R3PA

**Purpose:** Collects applications for the founding cohort of Futurelab FDE School. Every "Apply" button on the landing page should open this link.

**Header image:** `forms/google-form-header.jpg` (light theme, 4:1 ratio)

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

---

## Form description

> A 3-month programme for working engineers who want to become Forward Deployed Engineers. One live session a week. Taught by the team behind 50+ AI deployments across 75+ organisations. Fill in this short application and our team will reach out.

---

## Questions

| # | Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full name | Short answer | Yes |
| 2 | Email | Short answer | Yes |
| 3 | WhatsApp number | Short answer | Yes |
| 4 | Current role and company (e.g. Backend Engineer at XYZ) | Short answer | Yes |
| 5 | Years of experience | Dropdown | Yes |
| 6 | Which languages and tools do you work with? (e.g. Python, React, SQL, AWS) | Short answer | Yes |
| 7 | What have you built with AI so far? Tick all that apply. | Checkboxes | Yes |
| 8 | What do you want from this programme? | Multiple choice | Yes |
| 9 | Can you attend one 2-hour live session a week, plus about 3 to 4 hours on your own? | Multiple choice | Yes |
| 10 | LinkedIn profile link | Short answer | No |
| 11 | Anything you'd like to ask us? | Paragraph | No |
| 12 | Would you like to subscribe to Futurelab Signal, our weekly newsletter on AI news and ideas? | Multiple choice | Yes |

### Answer options

**5. Years of experience:** Less than 1 / 1 to 3 / 3 to 5 / 5 to 8

**7. What have you built with AI so far?**
- Nothing yet
- Prompts with ChatGPT or similar
- Called an AI API from code
- Built a RAG or document Q&A
- Built an agent
- Deployed AI to production

**8. What do you want from this programme?**
- Move into an FDE role
- Use AI in my current role
- Build my own AI product
- Other (free text)

**9. Time commitment:** Yes / Not sure yet

**12. Newsletter:** Yes / No

## Settings

- Sign-in is not required, so anyone outside Futurelab can apply.
- Email is collected through question 2, not the built-in email setting.
- Responses are linked to a Google Sheet for follow-up.
