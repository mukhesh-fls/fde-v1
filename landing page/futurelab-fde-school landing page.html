Frequently asked questions

Something else? Write to hello@futurelabstudios.com

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?+
An engineer who works directly with a business, finds the real problem, builds the AI system that solves it, and stays until it works in daily use.
Do I need AI or machine learning experience?+
No. You need to be comfortable writing code. We teach the AI parts from the start and skip machine learning theory.
Can I do this alongside a full-time job?+
Yes. There is one 2-hour live session a week, plus about 3 to 4 hours on your own for short videos and a weekly build.
What will I build?+
A data extractor, a document assistant, a workflow agent, a live deployed app, and a capstone system based on a real-world client problem.
Who teaches the programme?+
Futurelab’s founders and our engineering team.
Is there a job guarantee?+
No. Top performers may get projects or offers from Futurelab and its partner organisations, and everyone leaves with a portfolio of working projects and a deployed capstone.
What does it cost, and can I pay in instalments?+
The founding cohort price is ₹35,000 + GST, down from ₹80,000. Instalment options will be shared soon.
What if I miss a live session?+
Every session is recorded, so you can catch up during the week.
How are applicants selected?+
Our team reviews every application, then invites shortlisted engineers to a short technical call.
When does the next cohort start?+
Dates will be announced soon. Apply now to be considered for the founding cohort.
